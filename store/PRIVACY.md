## Short version

The app collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, and no analytics. It does not send anything to the publisher or to any third party.

## What stays on your device

The app saves two small items in the storage of the app on your device:

- Your result for each daily puzzle (win, loss, or draw, the move count, and the board outcome). The app uses this to show your result again if you reopen it.
- A flag that says you have seen the tutorial.

This data never leaves your device. If you delete the app, the data is deleted with it.

## Network use

The app has no network features. It does not make web requests, load remote code, fonts, or images, or contact any server. It works in airplane mode.

## Sharing

The Share button opens the share sheet of your device with a short text summary of your result. You choose whether to share it and with whom. The app does not see where you send it.

## Third-party services

The app uses no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social-login service.
The app uses these Capacitor components, which work only on your device: Haptics, Share, and Splash Screen.

Apple and Google may collect diagnostic data if you opt in to share it with developers in your device settings. The publisher cannot identify you from this data. This is a feature of the platform. It is not part of the app.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Web version

The same game is also available as a website at https://ultimate-tic-tac-toe-theta-five.vercel.app. The website code also makes no network calls and sets no cookies. It saves the same two items in the storage of your browser. The company that hosts the website may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, as part of normal web hosting. Those logs are outside the app.
