## Common questions

**How does the daily puzzle work?**
Every player gets the same puzzle each day. A new one unlocks at 00:00 UTC.

**I already finished today's puzzle. Can I replay it?**
No. The app shows your saved result until the next puzzle unlocks.

**Where is my result saved?**
Only on your device. There is no account and no cloud backup. If you delete the app or erase your device, the results are gone.

**How do I share my result?**
Finish the puzzle, then tap Share Result.

**The rules confuse me.**
Tap How to Play on the main screen to see the tutorial again.

**Does the app collect my data?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy]([PRIVACY POLICY URL]).
